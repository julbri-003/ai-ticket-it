## Brand & Style

The design system translates the dramatic luxury, ritual, and tactile majesty of classical performing arts into a crisp, modern digital interface. Designed for seamless AI-assisted theatrical discovery and ticketing, it pairs dramatic crimson and rich velvet burgundy with ivory, warm parchment tones, and burnished gold accents. 

Rejecting nostalgic kitsch—such as sepia tints, worn paper textures, faux-distressed stamps, or typewriter typography—the visual language remains ultra-refined, editorial, and airy. Broad negative space, disciplined geometric alignments, razor-sharp vector stubs, and high-contrast typography evoke the grandeur of royal opera houses through an unmistakably contemporary lens.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy sets an aristocratic yet accessible tone through the deliberate tension between classic high-contrast serifs and rationalist Swiss sans-serif typography.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in **Playfair Display Bold (700)**. Key dramatic terms, performance names, and marquee phrases must be styled in italic (`font-style: italic`). In dark mode, prominent italic phrases take the `#ff4757` highlight color to command visual authority.
- **Body, UI, & Numerical Data:** Set in **Inter** (regular 400, medium 500, semibold 600) to ensure legibility when reading seat numbers, performance schedules, and pricing matrices.
- **Section Labels:** Set in uppercase **Inter (600)** with extended tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.12em`), always prepended by the decorative gold symbol (`✦ `) styled in `#8a6417` (light) or `#d4af37` (dark).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a responsive fluid grid system designed to let visual elements breathe like an uncrowded stage.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a max-width container of `1280px`, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and `2.5rem` (`40px`) margins. 
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer page margins.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Spatial rhythm relies strictly on the structured spacing scale (`space-xs` through `space-2xl`). Vertical separation between theatrical acts, event groupings, and ticket showcases should lean generously into `space-xl` and `space-2xl` to evoke an uncluttered, high-end playbill layout.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons heavy drop shadows in favor of crisp architectural layering, luminous ambient glows, and disciplined borders.

- **Surface Tonal Layers:** Depth is created through surface tonal stepping: the ivory background (`#faf7f2`) carries pure white cards (`#ffffff`), while deep dark mode steps upward from velvet obsidian (`#0f0b0d`) to deep stall burgundy-charcoal (`#1d1417`) and floating elevated tiers (`#2a1a1f`).
- **Ambient Shadow Character:** When cards or modals elevate, they cast a long, highly diffused ambient falloff: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(28, 20, 22, 0.08)` in light mode, and `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)` in dark mode.
- **Micro-Gleams & Outlines:** In dark mode, active components and premium ticket modules utilize an inner 1px border with a soft gradient gleam tinted with decorative gold at 20% opacity, replicating the soft light of stage chandeliers hitting dark velvet upholstery.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp architectural lines with tailored curvature and structural silhouettes:

- **Cards & Structural Containers:** Built with a `16px` to `18px` border radius (`rounded-lg`). Ticket containers feature a distinctive silhouette: opposing top and bottom semicircular cutouts (notches with an `8px` to `10px` radius) set along a vertical dashed divider line that segments the main pass from the detachable right-hand stub.
- **Form Controls & Text Inputs:** Calibrated precisely at a `12px` border radius to provide a modern, balanced architectural enclosure.
- **Interactive Controls (Pills):** All action buttons, chips, filter badges, seat status tags, and numerical counter badges utilize a full pill radius (`9999px`).
- **Iconography:** Clean vector outline icons (Lucide aesthetic) rendered strictly with an elegant `1.75px` stroke weight, ensuring crisp alignment with Inter's medium-weight glyphs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:** Fully rounded pill (`border-radius: 9999px`). Uses velvet burgundy (`#7a1e2c`) with ivory text (`#faf7f2`) in light mode, and vibrant crimson (`#c8102e`) with white text (`#ffffff`) in dark mode. Typography is Inter SemiBold (600), `14px`, vertically padded by `12px` with `24px` horizontal padding.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border matching the contextual divider token (`#e6dcd0` light, `#3a2a2e` dark). Text is primary text color, shifting to subtle gold upon hover.

### Theatrical Ticket Card
- Constructed with `18px` outer rounded corners and a flat surface (`#ffffff` light, `#1d1417` dark).
- Features a clean 1px border.
- The ticket layout features a primary details zone on the left, an inset vertical perforation rule (dashed `1.5px` stroke using divider tokens), anchored by symmetrical top and bottom circular punch-out notches.
- The right-hand stub section hosts the QR code, seat identifier, and a solid filled ticket stub background (`#b3122c` in dark mode, `#f3ece4` in light mode).

### Input Fields & Selects
- Framed in a distinct `12px` border radius with an explicit 1px stroke (`#8c7d80` in light, `#7a6a64` in dark).
- Inner fill uses canvas or subtle surface tones.
- Floating helper and field labels use `label-section` styling in Inter (600).
- Focus states transition the stroke smoothly to gold (`#8a6417` in light, `#d4af37` in dark) with a soft 2px outer gold aura (15% opacity).

### Chips, Pills & Filters
- Compact pill-shaped elements (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- Selected filters adopt the primary velvet tint with high-contrast text; unselected filters leverage a subtle surface fill (`#f3ece4` light, `#2a1a1f` dark) with a neutral border and secondary text color.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio controls use concentric nested circles; checkboxes feature a sharp `4px` corner radius.
- Active states feature a solid fill in `#7a1e2c` (light) or `#c8102e` (dark) with a pristine white checkmark or inner dot.

### Lists & Row Items
- Segmented by hairline horizontal rules (`1px` solid, `#e6dcd0` light, `#3a2a2e` dark).
- Seating rows and cast credits leverage Inter for structural tabular data, aligning labels to the left and performance metrics/pricing flushed to the right.
